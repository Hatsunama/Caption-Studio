## Fixtures and invariants

Use a small saved project with two distinguishable videos, nonzero source trims, a gap/transition, unequal rates, extracted and independent overlapping audio, primary and dual captions (including overlapping cues), PiP, and text/image/watermark layers. Add an unsaved script/dual draft variant. Record stable IDs, counts, text, source references, trims/rates, timeline starts/ends, track order, lane mapping, selected owner, canvas transform and durable revision. Test at zero, a nonzero time, near a clip boundary and near the end; five loops alone do not cover these states.

- **Transport versus ruler:** record transport `currentMs`, native displayed horizontal offset `x`, duration `D`, track width `W`, viewport/content sizes and geometry generation separately. `timeline-scale.ts` maps `x = clamp(t/D, 0, 1) * W`, inverse `t = clamp(x/W, 0, 1) * D`. Use the timeline's real padding/label geometry, not screen-left as zero. Once follow owns the viewport and native geometry is ready, require agreement within one physical pixel converted to time (`D/W/PixelRatio` ms), plus measured playback sampling delay. Record that delay; a rounded clock alone cannot prove agreement. A selected cue, scrub or reorder may intentionally own the viewport; record that exception and require follow to recover when ownership ends. Programmatic reflow must not seek the transport to an old offset. Verify before pressing Play, which can mask a failure.
- **Edits/counts/lanes:** interruption alone changes no IDs, counts, text, visibility, track order, source links or timing. An intended cut adds exactly its domain-defined items once; extract/import adds only the accepted objects once. Compare expected operation deltas rather than demanding unchanged counts for successful edits. Unchanged timing/visibility/order retains lane assignment and label/item alignment. A timing change may legitimately repack lanes; compare against the same committed fixture. No disappearing or duplicated cues after reflow or reopening.
- **Coordinates/preview:** ordinary clip source time is `sourceStartMs + (timelineMs - clipTimelineStartMs) * playbackRate`; transition windows use the controller's transition mapping. Audio and PiP use their own trims/rates/offsets. Verify correct source/frame and audible region, including gaps, instead of equating source time with transport time. After canvas reflow use its current origin/dimensions; normalized placement, crop, rotation, scale and hit ownership remain consistent. No preview-only draft persists accidentally.
- **Ownership:** one touch stream owns one target captured by ID; pending touch is not an edit. Reflow/CANCEL invalidates stale samples and releases locks, timers and scroll dependencies once. No old up callback commits after cancellation; normal release applies the final sample once, and a fresh touch works. Check native/UI/RN queues, late layout/content/scroll acknowledgements, player-slot tokens, unmount/remount and replacement project/track lifetimes. A stale callback cannot select, seek, save or clear recovery for the new owner.
- **Jobs/errors:** enumerate pending seek/prepare/waveform/import/extract/record/transcribe/translate/download/verify/export/write jobs. Each completion belongs to its project, revision and attempt. Foreground return cannot duplicate work, auto-replace authored text, or claim successful cancellation while native stop is unresolved/failed. Preserve the real failure and accepted partial results, expose retry, prevent concurrent ownership until settlement, and clean only unreferenced app-owned temporaries. Linked originals and permissions still used elsewhere must survive.
- **Save/recovery:** distinguish in-memory edit, accepted durable save and recovery-journal receipt. A failed write retains edits/recovery and reports failure; retry saves the latest revision. A late successful receipt cannot erase newer typing. Reopen gives a complete old-or-new durable checkpoint, never torn/mixed state; unsaved draft recovery is credited only after its journal receipt. Check restore/keep/discard, unreadable primary/previous copy, and stale dialog responses. No promise that arbitrary unjournaled keystrokes survive death. MP4 success requires verified publication; late cancellation/share failure must not relabel a saved video as cancelled. SRT/ASS share return is not proof an external recipient saved a file.
