# Caption Studio store listing

## App name

Caption Studio

## Short description

Create local captions and style every word directly on your Android phone

## Full description

Caption Studio is a local-first video editor built around fast, flexible automatic captions.

Import one video or a whole sequence, generate multilingual captions on your phone, correct the script and timing, then control how captions look, move, and animate. Ordinary caption generation does not require an account, API key, transcription credits, or a cloud upload.

CAPTIONS THAT STAY EDITABLE

• Generate multilingual captions locally with downloadable Fast, Balanced, and Accurate models
• Optionally show English and Simplified or Traditional Chinese at the same time
• Keep paired lines linked when either language is edited, with local refresh and human-review status
• Edit the complete timestamped script
• Split, join, delete, and resize caption blocks on a layered timeline
• Keep word timing aligned through clip trims, cuts, gaps, and speed changes
• Export a finished MP4 with captions burned in, or export SRT and ASS subtitle files

ONE FONT LIBRARY

• Browse a varied built-in font collection in one place
• Search, favorite, and revisit recent fonts
• Import your own TTF and OTF fonts
• Apply a change to one subtitle or every subtitle
• Add unlimited extra text layers with the same typography controls

CREATIVE CONTROL

• Use active-word, karaoke, pop, bounce, punch, emoji, and other caption animations
• Choose from dozens of video transitions
• Adjust text and images with direct resize, rotate, and position controls
• Add image stickers from your phone and choose exactly when each layer appears
• Reorder visual layers to control what appears on top

VIDEO AND AUDIO EDITING

• Import multiple clips and keep playback moving across the full timeline
• Trim, split, reorder, rotate, crop, fit, fill, resize, and change playback speed
• Mute individual video clips and add separate audio files
• Extract audio from another video and place it on the audio timeline
• Use clip volume and fade controls
• Work in Original, 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, or 4:5 canvas formats

OPTIONAL ON-DEVICE BACKGROUND REMOVAL

Replace the area behind a person with an image or video without requiring a physical green screen. Resize, rotate, move, or animate the person along a motion path. Processing runs on the Android device; the app explains Google SDK operational metrics before this optional feature is enabled.

Caption Studio has no watermark, export quota, font pack paywall, or transcription-credit system. Projects are nondestructive, so your original selected media is never deleted or modified.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. Optional natural English–Chinese translation uses one additional approximately 1.6 GB local model, is not available until the user enables it, and can still require human review. Performance depends on video resolution, duration, selected model, and phone hardware.
